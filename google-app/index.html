Kaii

A personal anime library app for one person's own computer.

Kaii keeps track of the anime on one person's computer: what's downloaded, what's been watched, and each show's opening and ending songs. It is not a public service and has no accounts of its own.

With the owner's permission, Kaii uses the YouTube Data API for one thing: when its owner clicks "Add to playlist" next to a song, it adds that song's video to one YouTube playlist the owner chose. It does nothing on YouTube otherwise.

Kaii privacy policy

Kaii is a desktop app used by one person, its owner, on their own computer.

What Kaii accesses on Google

When its owner connects YouTube, Kaii asks for permission to manage their YouTube account (the youtube scope). It uses that permission only to add a video to the one playlist the owner chose, and to read that playlist's contents so the same video isn't added twice. Nothing is added without the owner clicking "Add to playlist".

Where the data goes

The sign-in tokens Google issues are stored only on the owner's computer. Kaii has no server: nothing from Google is sent to, stored by, or shared with anyone else, and nothing is used for advertising.

Removing access

The owner can disconnect YouTube in Kaii's settings, or remove Kaii's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Kaii's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
